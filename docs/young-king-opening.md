# Opening direction: before the crown

The revised opening fantasy starts with one persistent adolescent protagonist
who wants to become king. The player first learns to care about that person,
then meets the future queen, then the future general. Completing that opening
unlocks the full Phalanx campaign.

This is a design direction, not a shipped solo campaign. Today's New Game still
creates three founders. The visual/physics pass does not silently replace it
with a shortened adult model or claim a completed narrative chapter.

## Sequence and consequences

| Chapter | Playable group | What the player learns | Persistent consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Before the crown | One aspiring king | Observe autonomous choices, experiment with temperament and basic equipment, survive an attainable encounter | A stable identity, first practice, memories and a personal ambition; no royal stat bonus or crown yet |
| Meeting the future queen | Two people | Complementary tactics, disagreement, helping and dependence | A specific shared history; their bond is earned from actual encounters rather than assumed from office |
| Meeting the future general | Three people | Formation, protection, coordination and composition | Three distinct relationships, shared experiences and a recognizable team |
| Becoming a Phalanx | Three founders | Preparation, rival teams, reports and long-term progression | The complete campaign opens with these same veterans; subsequent matched 3v3 then 4v4 progression is retained |

Meeting a companion establishes that person's own identity and motivation. The
queen and general are future offices, not interchangeable class upgrades or
rewards purchased with currency. The player should remember how each person
joined, what went wrong and what they survived together.

The opening should introduce systems in play. It must not interrupt an early
solo encounter with every workshop, mastery, economy and AI inspection control.
Reports can reveal earned changes gradually, while detailed receipts remain
available for study.

## Dependencies before implementation

- A genuine adolescent visual/body treatment and explicit maturation; scaling
  the adult mesh down is not an implemented age system.
- Campaign roster and opponent generation that support a solo chapter and a
  two-person chapter without pretending either is the existing 3v3 league.
- Stable companion identities, join receipts, chapter saves and once-only
  transitions that preserve attributes, injuries, learned habits and histories.
- Relationship state with real behavioral consequences. A scripted meeting alone
  cannot claim the relationship-driven rescues in the development law.
- Separate earned offices from initial names and class mechanics. The current
  starting royal health/damage bonus should not apply before coronation.
- A replayable opening, clear failure/recovery behavior and migration that
  preserves established three-founder saves.

Early encounters should exercise actual simulation rules with bounded, readable
opponents. A narrative introduction may frame an encounter; it must not fabricate
combat success, legendary history or a rescue that did not occur.
