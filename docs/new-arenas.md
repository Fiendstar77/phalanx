# Three tactical fields

The Arena button / M cycles ten authored fields. New maps also accept CLI
`--arena gauntlet`, `--arena quarry`, and `--arena mire`. Founders retain their
persistent squads; the Lab uses ten fighters per side on these larger maps.

| Field | Layout | Challenge |
|---|---|---|
| Gauntlet | 50 x 40, four offset ruin walls | Route around switchback obstacles and avoid losing contact with allies in the choke points. |
| Quarry | 50 x 40, two raised ridges, central stone obstruction | Contest high ground or take the longer flank around the quarry stonework. |
| Mire | 50 x 40, two dry causeways and a muddy center | Compare direct slow movement against the longer dry routes. |

Walls, roughness and elevation are shared by route planning, movement, combat
and rendering. Differences are mechanical, not a different background image.
These remain local skirmishes, not a wave campaign or a new reward league.
The adaptive pressure-field generator remains available through Next battle;
manual Arena selection exposes the authored maps.

Tests validate spawns, sweep waypoints, connected routes, wall clearance during
battle, and enemy contact on each field. These checks establish playability,
not balanced outcomes across all team builds or policies.
