# A stranger world with persistent minds

Author direction, 2026-11-06: more individual faces and movement, extreme wuxia
agility, beasts, birds and mounts; a bizarre Phalanx identity broader than kabuki.
Persistent personalities, bonds, habits and rivalries lead this expansion. PyTorch
observations and experiments must develop alongside new simulation systems.

## Implemented first slice

Personal memory is directional and attached to stable fighter identity. Actual
healing, recognized incoming damage and witnessed screen positions have source
receipts. Reached flanks and recovery rallies teach bounded habits. Known helpers
can attract support even from an otherwise withdrawn fighter, influence medic and
screen selection, and color familiarity with recognized opponents. These effects
grant no combat statistics, unseen tracking, outcome-based temperament or legend.
The character details panel shows named recent memories.

Presentation adds 24 named facial blends, context selection and eight identity-
based movement signatures. Cadence, posture, hip/shoulder movement and phase vary;
injury and action-specific poses still take precedence. This extends the existing
clips rather than claiming 24 new sculpted morph targets or eight new motion clips.

Houndwarden fields one persistent companion through Pack Call. Steady Recall
reduces command effort. Hounds sense locally, report within communication range,
follow legal routes, attempt opposed bites, and can be repelled at the defender's
own effort/cooldown cost. Injury, recovery bouts, scars and recalled experience
persist. Their bodies are separate actors, not extra roster slots or victory
conditions. Hound damage retains its actual companion source. The companion's
independent AI is currently deterministic, not a neural animal policy.

Acrobat's Cloud Step requires trained Acrobatics, agility, effort and usable legs.
Its open-ground curved trajectory reaches a real landing; failed checks and
interruptions have separate receipts. Wall traversal remains a separate primitive.
This does not yet implement rooftop networks, aerial interception, enemy throws,
free flight or a complete multi-step wuxia planner.

The Ossuary Glaive, Funeral Bell Mace, Silk Lamellar and Porcelain Harness add
reach, blunt/cutting consequences and protection/mobility tradeoffs. Recipe costs
are in-game only. The new brain experiment is documented in
[the evidence report](results/personality-v1/README.md).

## World and next dependencies

Author direction for the hounds: pug-sized, adorable and necrotic; whether they
are undead remains undecided. A future master Houndwarden could command four or
five persistent animals, with distinct identities and coordinated scouting,
guarding, flanking and recall. Pack capacity and coordination need their own
practice, command and care rules. This is future work; the current handler has
one companion.

Kabuki contributes posture, staging and one cultural vocabulary. The world should
also develop anatomical geology, ritual industry, strange husbandry and living
infrastructure. The hound is the first new creature, not evidence that all these
cultures and ecologies already exist. Strong silhouettes and consistent causes
should tie regions together without making them copies of one theatrical style.

War birds need flight volumes, occlusion and bounded reports. Mounts need combined
mass, rider/animal control, traversal clearance, dismounts and separate injury.
Both should use persistent identities and witnessed relationships. They are future
work; neither is a cosmetic shortcut around absent movement or intelligence rules.

Next AI work should test relationship-driven intervention and coordination in
controlled counterfactual worlds, add opponent-visible temporal models, and then
learn companion/primitive proposals within the existing legality checks. Complexity
must be demonstrated through interacting consequences and reproducible tests.
